**FeatureCard** — an emoji-led card with a title, blurb, and gold-check feature list. Used for audience/value props ("Homeowners", "Contractors").

```jsx
<FeatureCard
  icon="🏡"
  title="Homeowners"
  description="Instant access to verified, local professionals."
  features={["Completely free — forever", "Verified contractors only", "Real reviews"]}
/>
```
