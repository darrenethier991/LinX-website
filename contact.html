Our Story

LinX was founded in Barrie, Ontario in 2024 by Darren Ethier. Homeowners describe the job once; verified local contractors are matched and notified automatically.

The platform is automation-first — from posting a job to contractor onboarding to billing. Built and operated right here in Simcoe County.
