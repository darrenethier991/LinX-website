HailShield workflow

A disciplined sequence from assessment to sale-ready handoff.

Rapid assessment

Coordinate a mobile assessment or dealership-led intake process immediately after the event.

Vehicle intake & tagging

Link the VIN, lot location, observed severity, and expected repair path to each vehicle record.

Evidence & report package

Use standardized angles and timestamps to compile a clear damage summary and supporting photo evidence.

Repair & final delivery

Coordinate the repair plan, review completion, and issue a final status report for the dealership team.

Insurance-ready operations

Make the recovery record easier to review.

A structured record is not a claim guarantee. HailShield™ is designed to help teams organize photos, vehicle identifiers, damage categorization, repair estimates, and communication history. Coverage decisions, repair scope, and timing remain subject to the applicable insurer, adjuster, repair provider, and dealership processes.
