What she hunts

Twenty-plus automated checks across your site or app. Non-destructive, no logins attempted, nothing changed.

🕸️ Websites

  • Missing security headers (CSP, HSTS, clickjacking)
  • Expired or untrusted TLS certificates
  • Exposed .git, .env, backups & database dumps
  • Reachable admin & login panels
  • Possible XSS & SQL injection points
  • Open redirects & CORS misconfigurations
  • WordPress version leaks & user enumeration

🕷️ Android apps

  • Debuggable release builds
  • Exported components any app can reach
  • Dangerous permissions (SMS, location, mic…)
  • Hardcoded API keys & secrets (masked in reports)
  • Weak crypto — ECB, DES, MD5, SHA-1
  • Debug-key signing & cleartext traffic
