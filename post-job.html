Homeowner guide

How quotes work on LinX

Written quotes

Every contractor gives you a written quote before any work begins. It lists materials, labour, and foreseeable costs. No work starts until you approve it.

No surprise charges

If conditions change (e.g. hidden damage), your contractor provides a revised quote first. Nothing extra gets charged without your approval.

Warranty

Warranty terms are set by your contractor and spelled out in your written quote — ask what’s covered and for how long before approving. Your agreement is with the contractor, not LinX.

Quote validity

Each written quote states how long it’s valid for. Material prices move, so confirm the expiry with your contractor before approving.

Optional 10% contingency

Your contractor may ask, “Would you like to include a 10% contingency for unforeseen materials?” It’s optional, not a fee. Any part that isn’t used is not charged — it only covers real, approved extras.
