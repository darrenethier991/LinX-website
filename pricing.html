Questions

Straight answers

Is there a free option?

Yes. Free Until First Job provides job browsing and one accepted job during LINX onboarding. Unlimited also offers a separate two-day trial through Stripe checkout.

How do leads reach me?

Homeowner jobs are matched to your trades and service area automatically and appear in your dashboard the moment they're posted. Growth adds real-time SMS alerts so you can respond first.

Can I cancel or change plans?

You can contact LINX Services to discuss a change to your plan or billing access. Stripe securely processes recurring payment information for paid plans.

Do homeowners pay anything?

Never. Posting jobs and getting quotes is free for homeowners — contractor memberships are what keep the platform running.
